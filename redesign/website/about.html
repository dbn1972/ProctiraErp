Our mission

Education infrastructure as a public good

We believe every learner is counted, every educator is supported, and every policymaker has the data they need — when the software that runs education is open, shared, and built to last.

Our story

From one district's spreadsheet problem

CivitasOne started in 2023 in Bhubaneswar, when a small team of engineers volunteered to help a district education office reconcile attendance registers, scholarship lists, and exam results that lived in hundreds of disconnected spreadsheets. The pattern repeated in every district they visited: dedicated educators spending evenings re-typing the same data into different government portals.

Instead of building another proprietary portal, we made a different bet: education management software should be public infrastructure — open source, self-hostable, and owned by the institutions that depend on it. No lock-in, no per-student licence fees, no black boxes around children's data.

Today the platform runs the Government of Odisha pilot across 142 schools and more than 24,000 students, with deployments in preparation in two more states. Every line of code remains Apache-2.0 licensed, and every improvement funded by one government benefits all of them.

What we value

Principles we build by

Open by default

Public infrastructure deserves public code. We build in the open, document our decisions, and never gate safety or privacy features behind a paywall.

Designed for scale

From a single classroom to a national ministry — the same platform, engineered to grow without rewriting, re-procuring, or re-training.

Accountable with data

Student data is encrypted, audited, and governed by the institution — never sold, never mined, never used for anything but education.

Locally adaptable

Different states, different policies, different languages. Themes, plugins, and translations let each deployment feel like it was built at home.

Built for real conditions

Shared devices, intermittent power, 2G connectivity. We test where our users teach — not just in the office on fibre.

Educators first

Every feature is measured by one question: does this give a teacher more time with students? If not, it doesn't ship.
