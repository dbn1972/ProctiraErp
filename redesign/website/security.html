Trust & security

Security at the core

CivitasOne handles some of the most sensitive data a society holds — student records. Every layer of the platform is engineered, reviewed, and audited with that responsibility in mind.

Baseline controls

How we protect education data

These controls ship enabled by default on every deployment — cloud-managed or self-hosted.

Encryption at rest & in transit

TLS 1.2+ everywhere and AES-256 at rest. Centralized secret management with automatic rotation and least-privilege access to keys.

RBAC & audit logs

Fine-grained, role-based permissions per module and field. Every read of sensitive student data and every write is recorded in an immutable audit trail.

Data residency in India

Cloud tenants run exclusively in MeitY-empanelled Indian regions. Self-hosted deployments keep data wherever your policy requires — including air-gapped.

Vulnerability disclosure

A public, coordinated disclosure program with a 90-day standard window, safe-harbour language, and published advisories for every fixed issue.

SOC 2 roadmap

SOC 2 Type II attestation is underway for the managed cloud, building on existing ISO 27001-certified operations. Evidence packs available under NDA.

Backups & disaster recovery

Continuous WAL archiving, nightly verified snapshots, and quarterly restore drills. RPO under 5 minutes and RTO under 4 hours on managed cloud.

Responsible disclosure

Found something? Tell us first.

We appreciate good-faith security research. Email a report with reproduction steps and impact details to security@civitasone.org — we acknowledge within two business days, keep you informed through the fix, and credit researchers in our advisories.
