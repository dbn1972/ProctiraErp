Legal · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 April 2026 · Effective for all visitors and CivitasOne cloud users

This Privacy Policy describes how CivitasOne ("we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses personal data when you visit our public website or use the CivitasOne cloud services. Self-hosted deployments are governed by the operating institution's own privacy notice; the principles below reflect the platform's default behaviour.

Data we collect

We collect only what is needed to operate the service and meet legal obligations:

  • Information you provide directly — contact form submissions, support requests, account registration details, and information shared during sales conversations.
  • Information collected automatically — server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited), session identifiers, and the minimal cookies described in our Cookie Policy.
  • Information from authorised institutions — when a school or government deploys our cloud service, it provides the student, guardian, and staff records required for education administration. The institution remains the Data Fiduciary; we act as a Data Processor on its instructions.

How we use it

Personal data is used to:

  • operate, secure, and improve the platform;
  • respond to enquiries and provide customer and deployment support;
  • send service communications such as maintenance windows and security notices;
  • comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We do not use student data for advertising, profiling, or model training — ever. Where the GDPR applies, our lawful bases are contractual necessity, legitimate interests, legal obligation, and consent.

Sharing

We share personal data only with:

  • Sub-processors — infrastructure and SMS providers operating under data-processing agreements, listed in our trust pack and located in India for Indian government tenants;
  • Government authorities — where required by applicable law, after legal review and, where lawful, notice to the affected institution;
  • The deploying institution — your school, district, or ministry controls its own tenant data at all times.

We never sell personal data, and we never disclose student records to third parties for commercial purposes.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it. Default platform retention periods are published in the data-processing register — for example, attendance records are retained for the academic year plus three years, while audit logs roll over after eighteen months. Website enquiry data is removed within twenty-four months of the last contact.

Your rights under DPDP

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 gives you, as a Data Principal, the right to:

  1. Access — obtain a summary of the personal data being processed and the processing activities;
  2. Correction and erasure — have inaccurate data corrected and data that is no longer necessary erased;
  3. Grievance redressal — have your complaint addressed within the statutory timeline before escalating to the Data Protection Board of India;
  4. Nomination — nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

For students using a school deployment, requests are routed through the institution as Data Fiduciary; we provide the tooling to fulfil them. Visitors and cloud users can write to us directly at privacy@civitasone.org — we respond within seven days.

Contact the DPO

Our Data Protection Officer oversees this policy and all data-subject requests:

  • Email: dpo@civitasone.org
  • Post: Data Protection Officer, CivitasOne, 4th Floor, Tech Tower, Infocity Square, Bhubaneswar 751024, Odisha, India

If you believe we have not resolved your concern, you may lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India or your local supervisory authority.
