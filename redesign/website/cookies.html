Cookie Policy

Last updated 2 March 2026 · Applies to civitasone.org and its subdomains

This Cookie Policy explains how the CivitasOne public website and services use cookies and similar technologies. We use a deliberately small set — no third-party advertising trackers, ever.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let the site remember your actions and preferences over time so you don't have to repeat them on every visit — for example, keeping you signed in or remembering your language.

Cookies we use

Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals: when your browser sends one, analytics and marketing cookies are disabled automatically — no consent surface required.

Changes & contact
