Trust & compliance

Compliance, in plain language

CivitasOne is built to operate inside the privacy and accountability frameworks that govern education data around the world — and we explain what each one actually means for you.

Frameworks & standards

What we comply with — and why it matters

DPDP Act 2023

Aligned

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act sets the rules for collecting and processing personal data, with special safeguards for children. CivitasOne implements verifiable consent capture, purpose limitation, breach notification workflows, and Data Principal request handling out of the box.

What this means for youParents and students can see, correct, and erase their data on request — and your institution can prove it acted lawfully, with consent records and audit trails ready for the Data Protection Board.

GDPR

Ready

For deployments that touch EU residents, the platform supports GDPR's lawful-basis model: data minimisation, the full set of data-subject rights, processor agreements, and Standard Contractual Clauses for any cross-border transfer.

What this means for youIf your institution serves international students or partners with EU organisations, you can deploy CivitasOne without building a parallel privacy programme — export, rectification, and erasure tooling is built in.

FERPA-aligned

Aligned

The US Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act shapes global best practice for student records. CivitasOne mirrors its core ideas: education records belong to students and parents, disclosure requires consent or a legitimate educational interest, and every access is logged.

What this means for youTeachers see only their classes. Clerks see only their modules. Guardians see only their children. Nobody — including platform operators — browses student records without an audited reason.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Tested

All public-facing and teacher-facing screens are designed and tested against WCAG 2.2 Level AA: keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantics, 4.5:1 contrast, visible focus, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility regressions block releases.

What this means for youStaff and students with disabilities can use the platform independently — and government deployments meet GIGW and Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act obligations without retrofitting.
