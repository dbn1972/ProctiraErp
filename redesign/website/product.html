Student lifecycle

Every student record, complete and current

Admissions, enrolment, section allocation, transfers, and graduation live in one timeline per student. Aadhaar-linked identity, guardian details, and document vault included.

  • Online admissions with merit lists and seat matrices
  • Bulk promotion and inter-school transfer certificates
  • Special education and inclusion program tagging
Attendance

Attendance that works offline-first

Teachers mark a full class in under a minute — on shared tablets, low-end phones, or paper-sync mode for no-network days. Data reconciles automatically when connectivity returns.

  • One-tap class marking with biometric and RFID options
  • SMS alerts to guardians for unexplained absences
  • Mid-day meal counts generated from the same register
Assessments

From question paper to report card

Plan exams across boards and terms, capture marks in bulk, moderate grades with full audit history, and publish report cards parents can actually read — in English, Hindi, or Odia.

  • CBSE, ICSE, and state-board grading schemes built in
  • Continuous assessment (FA/SA) with weighted aggregation
  • Result analytics by subject, section, and teacher
Scholarships & DBT

Scholarships that reach the right students

Eligibility engines match students to central and state schemes automatically. Applications, verification, sanction, and DBT disbursal are tracked end to end — with zero duplicate payouts.

  • Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, and state schemes pre-configured
  • Aadhaar eKYC and bank seeding validation before sanction
  • PFMS-ready disbursal files and reconciliation reports
Analytics

Decisions backed by live data

Pre-built dashboards roll attendance, results, and finance up from classroom to ministry. Slice by district, block, gender, or scheme — and export everything to UDISE+ formats.

  • Drop-out risk signals from attendance and result trends
  • District league boards with drill-down to each school
  • Scheduled email and PDF reports for review meetings
