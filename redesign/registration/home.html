Who can apply?

Every child has a right to free education under the RTE Act 2009. Government schools in Odisha charge no admission fee, no tuition fee, and provide free textbooks, uniforms, and the mid-day meal.

  • Age: children who complete 6 years by 1 September 2026 can join Class 1. Older children are placed in an age-appropriate class.
  • Residence: any family living in Puri district. Priority is given to schools within 1 km of your home for primary classes.
  • Documents: birth certificate and address proof are needed. A caste certificate is optional and only used for scholarship benefits.
  • No document? Apply anyway. Admission cannot be denied for missing papers — the school will help you obtain them after enrollment.

Common questions

Quick answers from parents who applied before you.

Does it cost anything to apply or study?
No. Registration is free, admission is free, and education in government schools is free — including textbooks, two sets of uniforms, and the mid-day meal. If anyone asks you for money to "process" your application, please report it on the helpline 155335.
I don't have a birth certificate for my child. Can I still apply?
Yes. Start the application and skip the upload. An Anganwadi record, hospital record, or a self-declaration of age is accepted temporarily. The school will guide you on getting the certificate made after admission — it cannot be a reason to refuse your child.
Can I apply to more than one school?
Yes, you may select up to three schools in order of preference. If your first choice has no seats in that class, your application automatically moves to the next school on your list — you do not need to apply again.
How long does the whole process take?
Document verification takes 2–3 working days. The school then reviews the application and confirms the seat, usually within a week. You will receive an SMS at every step, and you can check the status anytime on the Track Application page.
